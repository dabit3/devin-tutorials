1. Devin can turn a screenshot or sketch into working UI.

2. Attach an image to your prompt, and Devin treats it like a spec: the layout, the labels, even the notes you scribbled on it.

3. Here, I've attached a rough sketch of a new Team page for our Orbit demo app.

4. I ask Devin to build it with the real data, then check it in the browser against the sketch.

5. Devin reads the codebase, builds the page with the app's own components, then opens it in its own browser and records itself testing it.

6. It clicks through the new page the way a user would.

7. Here's my sketch, and the page Devin built, running live.

8. Devin compares them for you, and tells you where it matched the sketch and where it changed things to fit the app.

9. Its test report has the recording, with every check marked as passed.

10. Each check is a chapter in the video, so you can jump right to it.

11. You can also open the running app yourself, right inside the session.

12. Here's the new Team page, with each person's open issues and labels.

13. Click a person, and the board filters to their issues, just like the note on the sketch said.

14. And the pull request is ready for review, with its checks passing.

15. Try it with a design mockup, a photo of a whiteboard, or a screenshot of a page you want to match.
